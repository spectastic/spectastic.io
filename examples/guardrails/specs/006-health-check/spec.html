1 · Context

The orchestrator needs liveness/readiness probes. This feature adds a health endpoint reporting whether the service and its dependencies are reachable. It is operational plumbing with no bearing on positions or ADR-0007.

2 · User scenarios & testing

Healthy. GET /health returns 200 when the store is reachable.

Degraded. An unreachable store returns 503.

3 · Requirements

The service MUST expose a health endpoint reporting liveness and readiness. Readiness MUST report unhealthy when the position store is unreachable. The probe SHOULD complete quickly and MUST NOT mutate any state.
  • Full metrics/observability — a later feature.
  • Distributed tracing — out of scope here.

4 · Data model

Health status
live/ready booleans + a reachability check of the store.

5 · Success criteria

The health endpoint returns 200 when the store is reachable and 503 when it is not.
Observed at

A test toggling store reachability and asserting the status code.
