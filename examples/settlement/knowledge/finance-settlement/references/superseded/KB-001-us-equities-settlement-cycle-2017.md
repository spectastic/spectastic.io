# US equities settlement cycle (T+2, superseded)

Under the 2017 amendment to SEC Rule 15c6-1, the US standard settlement cycle
was **T+2** — a standard US-equity trade settled **two business days** after its
trade date — effective **5 September 2017**.

This is the rule in force **before 28 May 2024**. It is retained here, never
overwritten, so an edition-pinned citation to the older cycle — `KB-001@2017-09-05`
— always resolves, and a trade struck before the cutover can still be settled
under the cycle that actually governed it.

Superseded by the `2024-05-28` (T+1) edition. This is a hand-authored
distillation, not a verbatim reproduction of the rule text.
