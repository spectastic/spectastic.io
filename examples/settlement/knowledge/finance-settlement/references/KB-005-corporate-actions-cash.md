# Corporate-action cash entitlements

For a **cash dividend**, the entitlement to the cash turns on three dates:

- **Ex-date** — from this date the security trades without the right to the
  declared dividend; a buyer on or after ex-date is not entitled.
- **Record date** — the holder of record on this date receives the dividend.
- **Pay date** — the date the cash is actually disbursed.

A corporate action falling **between a trade's trade date and its settlement
date** can collide with settlement — the buyer has traded but not yet settled
when the entitlement is struck — which is why entitlement is decided by these
dates, not by settled position alone.
